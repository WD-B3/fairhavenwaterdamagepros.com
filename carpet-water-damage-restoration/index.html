Carpet Water Damage Restoration and Drying in Fairhaven, MA

Carpet and pad can often be saved if the water was clean and drying starts quickly. The water type decides.

☎ Call (774) 483-4896

Carpet water damage in Fairhaven, MA is often more than the surface. The pad underneath holds water, and the subfloor can stay wet after the carpet feels dry. If the carpet is soaked, stop the source and call dispatch, which is open 24/7, for guidance on extraction and drying.

This page explains when carpet can be dried in place, when only the pad is replaced, and when the whole installation should be removed.

Clean water versus contaminated water

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard groups water by contamination. Clean supply line water is Category 1, gray water is Category 2, and sewage or flood water is Category 3. Carpet soaked with Category 3 water is usually removed and discarded.

Clean water carpet can often be extracted and dried in place if action comes quickly. Delay allows the water to become a bigger problem.

Wet carpet pad

Pad acts like a sponge and dries slowly under the carpet. Many crews pull back the carpet at one edge, remove wet pad and reinstall the carpet over new pad. Pad is inexpensive compared with the risk of mold in a soaked layer.

Waffle and rebond pads respond differently, and a moisture meter can show what is still wet.

Extraction and drying

Crews extract with a powerful wand, lift the carpet where needed and set air movers and dehumidifiers. Floor drying is checked with moisture meters under the carpet. See water extraction for equipment detail.

Rooms are closed during drying, and readings are logged until the subfloor matches a dry reference.

Odor and cleaning after drying

Once dry, carpet can be cleaned and deodorized. A musty smell that remains suggests moisture still trapped, so recheck readings. The structural drying page explains why surface dryness is not enough.

If you see dark spots, ask about mold remediation.

Insurance and what to keep

Photograph the carpet and pad before removal, save a sample of the carpet if the insurer wants to see it and keep invoices. Coverage depends on the cause and category of water.

Carpet age and type affect replacement scope, so note when it was installed.

Stairs, glue-down carpet and area rugs

Stairs and glue-down carpet are harder than a stretched room. Glue can fail, and moisture can sit between the carpet and concrete. Area rugs are often easier because they can be taken out, cleaned and dried flat.

Tell dispatch what kind of installation you have. It changes the plan for lifting, drying and reinstalling.

What to do first

Lift furniture off wet carpet, place foil or plastic under legs and remove small items. Do not use a household vacuum on wet carpet, and avoid walking on it to prevent spreading water.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can wet carpet be saved?

Often, if the water was clean and drying began quickly. Contaminated water usually means removal.

Do I need to replace the pad?

Frequently yes, because pad holds water and dries slowly.

How long before mold can grow?

Per the EPA, growth can often be avoided when wet materials are dry within one to two days, which is why speed counts.

Can I rent a carpet cleaner to dry it?

Cleaners extract surface water but do not dry the pad or subfloor. Readings are needed.
