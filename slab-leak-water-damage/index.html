Emergency Slab Leak Water Damage in Fairhaven, MA

A leak in a pipe under a concrete slab can be hard to see. If floors feel warm or wet, shut off the water and call the 24/7 line.

A slab leak in Fairhaven, MA is an emergency when water is coming up through a floor, and the dispatch line is open 24/7. Close the main water valve, keep people out of the wet area and avoid electrical equipment near it. Then have a plumber confirm where the line is leaking.

A slab leak is a leak in a water line that runs in or under a concrete slab. Not every house has a slab foundation, so check what your home sits on before assuming this is your problem. This page is useful when a plumber has found, or strongly suspects, a leaking line under a concrete floor.

Symptoms that point to a hidden slab leak

People searching for this usually describe symptoms before they know the name. A water meter that keeps moving with every fixture off, a floor that feels warm or damp, or a hissing sound under the floor all point to a hidden leak. Sudden water bill changes can also be a clue.

A plumber confirms with listening equipment or pressure tests. Restoration begins after the leak is found and stopped, because drying cannot finish while water keeps feeding the slab.

What water under a slab does

Water that leaks under or through a slab can wet the subfloor material above it, the baseboard, the bottom of walls and anything stored on the floor. It can lift laminate, damage carpet and soften adhesive.

Concrete itself holds moisture and releases it slowly, so floors above it can stay damp long after the pipe is fixed. Monitoring with moisture meters tells you when it is safe to put new flooring down.

Repair options are a plumber's decision

Plumbers choose between spot repair through the slab, rerouting the line through walls or attic space and replacing the line. Each approach has trade-offs in cost, mess and permanence. Your plumber can say whether the repair needs a permit, and the Fairhaven Building Department is the office that handles plumbing permits.

Restoration begins when the line is repaired or rerouted, and the repair work may create more debris that crews have to account for.

Drying the floor and walls

Crews open baseboards, remove wet carpet and pad and set air movers and dehumidifiers. Hardwood or laminate is checked for cupping and swelling. Drying a slab environment can take longer than drying a wall cavity because concrete holds water.

For floor decisions see the hardwood floor page and the carpet page, which explain which finishes recover.

Local housing note

No official source used for this site states how many Fairhaven homes are built on slabs, so this page does not assume any local frequency. The town's hazard mitigation plan notes nearly 3,000 homes built before 1940, so check whether your own house sits on a slab, a basement or a crawl space before assuming anything.

If your home has a slab, usually on additions, garages or newer construction, the concerns on this page apply. If it has a basement or crawl space, see the crawl space page instead.

Safety checklist for a suspected slab leak

A slab leak can mean a pressurized line under concrete, so water can appear in more than one place and warm floors can signal a hot water line. The Massachusetts Plumbing Code (248 CMR) governs repair work, and a plumber handles the pipe.

Meanwhile, protect the house from the water and the people in it.

  • Close the main shutoff to stop the flow.
  • Stay off warm or wet floors near outlets.
  • Move furniture off damp flooring.
  • Note where the floor feels warm or damp.
  • Call a plumber and the 24/7 line.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you handle slab leak water damage emergencies at night or on weekends in Fairhaven?

Yes. Water coming up through a floor is urgent, and the Fairhaven line is open 24/7. Call after shutting the main valve, and dispatch arranges the earliest response it can for the water damage while your plumber handles the line.

Why is my floor warm and my water bill high?

Those two signs together can point to a leaking hot water line under a slab or floor. Shut off the water heater if you can, check whether the meter moves with every fixture off and call a plumber for detection.

Do all Fairhaven homes have slabs?

No. Many older homes have basements or crawl spaces. Check your own foundation type.

Who finds the leak?

A plumber with leak detection equipment finds it. Restoration begins once the line is repaired or rerouted.

Can drying start before the pipe is fixed?

Standing water can be removed, but full drying waits until the leak is stopped.

How long does the floor stay wet?

Concrete holds moisture, so moisture readings, not appearance, show when drying is finished.
