Water Damage Restoration in North Fairhaven, MA

Older two-family houses and cottages where one unit's leak often becomes the neighbor's ceiling.

☎ Call (774) 483-4896

North Fairhaven is the town's densely built northern neighborhood. The hazard mitigation plan describes it as densely populated with older two-family homes, and the state historical survey adds Italianate villas at Main and Coggeshall Streets, small cottages and bungalows from the 1920s, and three-deckers along Adams Street.

That mix shapes the water damage calls from here. Two-family and multi-unit buildings share floors, walls and plumbing runs, so a leak seldom stays in one apartment.

Why two-family buildings are different

When two households share a building, the first call is often about water coming through the ceiling from the unit above. The source may be a supply line, a drain, an appliance or a tub in a home you cannot enter.

Tenants should photograph the damage and notify the landlord in writing. The appliance leak page and the ceiling water damage page cover the two most common sources.

What a crew checks in a shared building

The visit is organized around the path the water took, not just the room where it showed up.

QuestionWhy it matters
Where did the water enter?The wet room is often not the source room.
Which units and walls are wet?Moisture can cross floors and party walls.
What kind of water is it?Clean supply water and drain water need different handling.
Who needs the records?Owners, tenants and insurers each ask for documentation.

Houses from the 1920s and earlier

The survey notes small hip and gambrel cottages, bungalows and simple two-family houses built in the 1920s, and Greek Revival and Italianate houses from the earlier period. Some have basements and some do not, so crews confirm what each building has instead of assuming.

If a cellar is wet, read the basement water damage page. If growth followed a leak, see the mold remediation page, which follows EPA guidance on finding and fixing the moisture source first.

Main Street and the commercial strip

The hazard mitigation plan also describes a struggling commercial center in North Fairhaven. Small shops and apartments above them share the same risks, and the commercial water damage page explains how crews document a business loss.

Safe steps before help arrives

Close the shutoff that serves the leak, and keep everyone out of water that touches outlets or appliances. Do not stand in water to reach a breaker, and leave sewage to trained crews. Then call the line and describe the building: how many units, which floor and where the water is showing.

Frequently Asked Questions

The leak is in the apartment above mine. What should I do?

Photograph your damage, notify the landlord in writing and call. Crews can start drying your unit, but the source above must be stopped by whoever controls that unit.

Who pays when a neighbor's pipe damages my unit?

That depends on the leases, the cause and each party's policy. Ask your insurer and keep every record. This site cannot say who is responsible in a specific case.

Do you work in three-deckers and two-families?

Yes. Crews dry multi-level buildings by tracing the water from the source down through each floor.

Can drying happen while neighbors are home?

Usually yes, though equipment makes noise and uses power. Dispatch explains the setup before work begins.
