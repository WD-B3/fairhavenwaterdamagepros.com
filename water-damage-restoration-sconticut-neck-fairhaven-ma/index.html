Water Damage Restoration in Sconticut Neck, Fairhaven, MA

A peninsula of shore homes that the town's own reports place outside the hurricane barrier.

☎ Call (774) 483-4896

Sconticut Neck is the peninsula that reaches into Buzzards Bay south of the town center. The state historical survey describes resort development here in the late 1800s and early 1900s, with summer houses along Sconticut Neck Road and smaller houses and bungalows later.

The hazard mitigation plan says many such shore homes have been elevated and converted to year-round residences. For restoration, the main point is exposure: the town's reports treat this peninsula differently from the rest of Fairhaven.

Outside the hurricane barrier

The town's climate vulnerability assessment says Sconticut Neck, West Island and Shaw's Cove sit seaward of the New Bedford Hurricane Barrier and experience unmitigated coastal storms. The hazard mitigation plan says the Fairhaven Dike protects all of town except Sconticut Neck, West Island and the northern shore of Nasketucket Bay.

A note on flood zones

The hazard mitigation plan also says most of the town's floodplain development is on West Island and Sconticut Neck. That is a statement about the town. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Look up the exact address on the FEMA flood map.

Storm water versus plumbing water

Shore homes get two kinds of water trouble. Storm flooding and wind-driven rain come from outside, and burst pipes, water heaters and appliances come from inside. Each has a different treatment, and floodwater is treated as contaminated.

Read the flood damage cleanup page and the storm water damage page for the exterior case. Crawl spaces under raised homes are covered on the crawl space page.

Seasonal houses and winter plumbing

Cottages that sat empty for part of the year can have supply lines that leak or freeze unseen. If a house has been vacant, enter carefully, check for standing water and shut the main before turning anything on. The frozen pipe page explains the thaw risk.

What to do during and after a storm

Protect yourself first. Do not wade through floodwater, which can hide hazards and may carry contamination. Keep out of water that touches outlets or appliances, and do not touch the panel while standing in water. If the area is under an evacuation order, follow it.

When it is safe, photograph the damage, move belongings up and call the line. The neighboring West Island page covers the other side of the causeway.

Insurance wording to know

According to the Massachusetts Division of Insurance, standard homeowners policies often exclude flood and sewer backup unless added. Read your policy early, and note that coverage questions go to your insurer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Sconticut Neck protected by the hurricane barrier?

Town reports say it is not. The climate vulnerability assessment places it seaward of the barrier.

Is my Sconticut Neck home in a FEMA flood zone?

Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Use the FEMA flood map for the exact address or ask the Fairhaven Building Department.

Can you help after a coastal storm?

Yes, once it is safe to reach the property. Dispatch asks about road access and the water type before planning the visit.

Do you handle seasonal home leaks?

Yes. Describe how long the house sat empty and where the water is showing.

Should I clean floodwater myself?

No. Floodwater is treated as contaminated. Photograph it and leave removal to trained crews.
