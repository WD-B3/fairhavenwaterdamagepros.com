Water Damage Restoration in Oxford Village, Fairhaven, MA

A former shipbuilding village with older cottages, bungalows and two-family houses.

☎ Call (774) 483-4896

Oxford Village began as a shipbuilding settlement. The state historical survey says shipbuilding began there before 1775 and that a second shipyard started in 1781. After an 1798 bridge caused silting that closed its harbor, the village was eclipsed by the Center, and a working-class neighborhood developed.

Today the housing described in the survey is mostly modest. Our crews meet it with the same method used anywhere in Fairhaven: find the source, extract, dry with readings and document.

Housing the survey describes

For the northern half of Fairhaven, the survey lists two-family houses, cottages and bungalows from the late 1800s and early 1900s. It also records some three-deckers, including on Adams Street at Oxford, and the Oxford School of 1896, a red brick Colonial Revival building. Other sources report that the school was later converted to apartments.

Small houses, shared walls

In a cottage or bungalow, a single leak can reach most of the living space quickly. In a two-family house, it can reach the neighbor too. The questions below decide how crews plan.

  • Where is the water coming from, and has it been stopped?
  • How many rooms or units are wet?
  • Is the water clean, or does it involve a drain or sewage?
  • Are ceilings, floors or wall cavities holding water?

See the water extraction page for the first visit, and the sewage backup page if a drain is involved. Do not clean sewage yourself.

Flooding in Oxford Village

We found no source that documents present-day flooding specific to Oxford Village. The silting story is historical and does not describe current conditions. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified.

If you want to know whether your property sits in a mapped flood hazard area, use the FEMA flood map or ask the Fairhaven Building Department.

Steps before the crew arrives

Close the main shutoff, lift belongings off wet floors and keep people and pets out of any water that touches electrical items. Avoid standing in water to reach the panel. Photograph everything, then call and describe the leak. Mold can follow water that sits, so quick drying matters. The mold remediation page explains the careful way to treat growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Oxford Village a separate town?

No. It is a village within Fairhaven, covered by the 02719 ZIP code.

Do you work in older cottages and bungalows?

Yes. Crews dry small and large houses alike, and they inspect what is in the walls and floors rather than guess.

My upstairs neighbor's leak is damaging my ceiling. What now?

Photograph the damage, tell your landlord in writing and call. Crews can dry your unit while the source is fixed.

Will I see a written scope?

Dispatch explains scope and price before work starts, and you keep photos and moisture logs for your insurer.
