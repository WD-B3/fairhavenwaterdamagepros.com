The best way to reach us is by phone. Call (774) 483-4896 and dispatch answers around the clock, every day of the year. There are no forms on this site, so a call is the way to start.

If anyone is in danger from fire, gas, electrical shock or rising water, call 911 first.

How to reach Fairhaven Water Damage Pros

Phone: (774) 483-4896 (24/7 dispatch)

Office: 9 Plaza Way, Fairhaven, MA 02719

What to have ready when you call

  • Your address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water came from and whether it has stopped.
  • How far the water has spread and which rooms are affected.
  • Whether anyone has turned off the water or power.
  • Your insurer, if you know it, and whether you own or rent.
