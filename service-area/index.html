Service Area: Fairhaven and Nearby Towns

Based in Fairhaven and serving five nearby towns on the South Coast, with a 24/7 emergency line.

☎ Call (774) 483-4896

Fairhaven Water Damage Pros is based at 9 Plaza Way in Fairhaven and serves five nearby communities. Each page below explains what water damage looks like in that town, which ZIP codes it covers and what to do while you wait for help.

The 24/7 emergency line is the same everywhere.

Towns we serve

New Bedford, MA

The closest neighbor, across the Acushnet River. Harbor flooding, older multi-family buildings and commercial waterfront.

Bristol County. ZIP codes: 02740, 02741, 02742, 02744, 02745, 02746.

Mattapoisett, MA

A Buzzards Bay town in Plymouth County with harbor homes, seasonal cottages and wind-driven storms.

Plymouth County. ZIP codes: 02739.

Acushnet, MA

A town north of Fairhaven with older houses, high groundwater in places and many basements.

Bristol County. ZIP codes: 02743.

North Dartmouth, MA

A section of Dartmouth with rentals near the university, family homes and Route 6 businesses.

Bristol County. ZIP codes: 02747.

Marion, MA

A small town at the head of Buzzards Bay with harbor streets, crawl spaces and coastal storm exposure.

Plymouth County. ZIP codes: 02738.

Fairhaven itself is covered from the office at 9 Plaza Way, ZIP code 02719, including Fairhaven Center, East Fairhaven, North Fairhaven, Sconticut Neck, Poverty Point, Nasketucket and Winsegansett Heights.

People also ask about Dartmouth, Westport and Rochester. Call the line and describe where you are, and dispatch will tell you what is possible. Those places do not have their own pages here.

How coverage works

Every call starts with the address. Dispatch confirms the town, checks whether a crew can reach it and gives safety steps while the visit is arranged. Coverage is described by town and ZIP code so you can see quickly whether your property is on the list.

Towns on opposite sides of a county line can have different building departments and utilities. Fairhaven, New Bedford, Acushnet and North Dartmouth are in Bristol County, and Mattapoisett and Marion are in Plymouth County.

Local conditions that shape the work

The coastline from Fairhaven to Marion takes storms off Buzzards Bay, so wind-driven rain, tidal flooding and nor'easters appear on many pages. Inland towns such as Acushnet deal more with groundwater, basements and aging pipes.

Older housing is common everywhere on the list, which is why leaks inside walls, cellars that stay damp and failing supply lines come up so often. Each town page explains what that means for its own streets.

Fairhaven neighborhoods inside the home office area

Within Fairhaven itself, crews work in Fairhaven Center near Town Hall and the Millicent Library, in East Fairhaven, in North Fairhaven, in Oxford Village, on Sconticut Neck and West Island, and in the villages of Poverty Point, Nasketucket and Winsegansett Heights.

Shoreline properties near Fort Phoenix and on Sconticut Neck deal with tide and wind, while homes in the older village streets deal more with aging plumbing and cellars. Both are common reasons for a call.

Questions we hear from each town

New Bedford callers ask about harbor flooding and older multi-family buildings. Mattapoisett and Marion callers ask about seasonal homes, storms and septic systems. Acushnet callers ask about basements and groundwater, and North Dartmouth callers ask about rentals, appliances and Route 6 businesses.

The town pages answer those questions in detail, with the local facts and the offices to contact. If your question is not covered, call and ask.

Insurance and paperwork across towns

Insurance rules come from your policy, not your town, but permits and utilities do change from town to town. Keep photos, moisture logs and receipts wherever you live, and ask your local building department or water utility about its own requirements.

The office is at 9 Plaza Way in Fairhaven, ZIP code 02719, and crews travel from there. Distances to the nearby towns are short, but traffic over the New Bedford-Fairhaven Bridge, Route 6 and Interstate 195 can change how long a trip takes, which is why no arrival time is promised on this site.

Before you call

  • Note your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Find out where the main shutoff is and close it if water is running.
  • Photograph the damage and the source.
  • Know your insurer and whether you own or rent.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do some towns have pages and others do not?

The pages cover the five nearby towns where we have the most local detail to share. Other places may still be reachable, so call and ask.

Do you serve towns not listed here?

Call the line and describe where you are. Dispatch will tell you whether the address can be reached.

Which county is each town in?

Fairhaven, New Bedford, Acushnet and North Dartmouth are in Bristol County. Mattapoisett and Marion are in Plymouth County.

Does a different county change anything?

It can change which building department handles permits. Ask your town office.
