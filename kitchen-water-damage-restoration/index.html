Kitchen Water Damage Restoration and Repair in Fairhaven, MA

Cabinets, countertops and flooring hide water. We find where it went and dry it before it spreads.

☎ Call (774) 483-4896

Kitchen water damage in Fairhaven, MA often starts under the sink, behind the dishwasher or at a refrigerator line, and it spreads into cabinet bases, flooring and walls before it is obvious. If water is actively leaking, close the shutoff valve under the sink, and call dispatch, which is open 24/7, if it has reached the floor.

This page covers the kitchen as a room, including cabinets, countertops, sink areas and flooring. The failure of an appliance itself is covered on the appliance leak page.

Water under the sink and behind cabinets

The area under a kitchen sink holds supply lines, a drain trap and often a disposal. Slow drips here soak the cabinet floor, which is often particleboard that swells and crumbles when wet. Water can also run along the wall into adjacent cabinets.

Look for swollen cabinet bottoms, a musty smell, soft flooring near the sink and stains on the wall. If you see any of these, shut off the water to the fixture.

Cabinets: dry, repair or replace

Solid wood boxes can often be dried, while particleboard and laminate typically swell and lose strength. Crews check cabinet bases and toe-kicks with moisture meters and open them to see behind them. Doors and drawer fronts are often fine even when the box is damaged.

Countertops stay in place when possible, but removal may be needed to reach wet cabinets below.

Kitchen floors

Water spreads under vinyl, laminate and tile. Laminate and vinyl planks can lift, and tile may hide wet subfloor. Hardwood can cup. The hardwood floor page explains what recovers.

Crews use meters and thermal imaging to follow water under flooring and cabinets, then dry with air movers and dehumidifiers.

Appliance leaks and supply lines

Dishwasher and refrigerator lines, ice makers and faucets are frequent sources. If a failed appliance caused the loss, the appliance leak page explains the sequence and renter or landlord questions.

Replace old rubber supply lines with braided ones and check shutoff valves for drips each year.

Drying, documentation and rebuild

Moisture readings are logged, and equipment stays until materials match dry references. Photos of cabinets, flooring and the source help your claim. A plumber repairs the leak, and a contractor handles cabinet and flooring replacement if needed.

If the leak ran long, ask about mold testing, and see mold remediation for how testing and removal are handled.

Dishwashers, ice makers and freezers

Not every kitchen leak starts at the sink. A dishwasher door gasket or drain hose can leak a little each cycle, an ice maker line can drip behind the refrigerator and a defrosting freezer can spill. These leaks hide behind appliances and under cabinets.

Pull appliances gently if you suspect a leak, and put a towel under the hoses to see where the dripping starts.

Safety checklist for a wet kitchen

Kitchens combine water, electricity and gas, so check each before you start cleanup. If you smell gas, leave and call 911 and the gas utility from outside.

Food that touched floodwater or sewage is not safe to keep, and dishes that sat in contaminated water need washing and sanitizing.

  • Close the shutoff under the sink or the main valve.
  • Unplug appliances only from a dry spot.
  • Never run a wet vacuum near a wet outlet.
  • Discard food that touched contaminated water.
  • Photograph cabinets and flooring before removal.

What shapes the cost of kitchen water damage

Kitchens combine expensive materials in a small space, so scope depends on what got wet.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I have to replace all my cabinets?

Not always. Meters show which boxes are wet, and many can be dried while others need replacement.

Can countertops stay?

Often yes. Removal depends on whether water reached the cabinet and subfloor below.

What if the floor feels soft?

Soft flooring suggests water below the surface. Stop using the area and have it assessed.

Who fixes the sink or drain?

A plumber repairs the plumbing. Restoration handles drying and repair of water damage.
