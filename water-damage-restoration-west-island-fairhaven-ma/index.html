Water Damage Restoration in West Island, Fairhaven, MA

An island of shore homes reached by a causeway the town has flagged for storm flooding.

☎ Call (774) 483-4896

West Island sits at the town's easternmost reach into Buzzards Bay and joins the mainland by way of Sconticut Neck. The town's climate vulnerability assessment describes Goulart Memorial Drive as a causeway between Sconticut Neck and West Island.

The hazard mitigation plan says West Island and Sconticut Neck hold summer homes and cottages, many converted to year-round residences. Fewer sources cover West Island than the Center, so this page sticks to what they say.

The causeway flooded in December 2022

The assessment reports that a storm on December 23, 2022 flooded low-lying areas outside the hurricane barrier, especially Goulart Memorial Drive. It also lists the causeway among low-lying roads at risk of storm flooding and among roads that may serve as evacuation routes from more distant parts of town.

The report does not name the storm. We cite the date, not any damage figure, since none is given.

Outside the barrier

Both the assessment and the hazard mitigation plan place West Island outside the barrier's protection. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified. Check the FEMA flood map for your own address, and read the flood damage cleanup page to see how crews handle floodwater.

Water types and what to do

Not all water is alike, and the type decides the handling.

Water sourceHow it is treated
Burst pipe or supply leakGenerally clean at first, dried quickly with structural drying.
Rain and wind-driven waterTreated as outside water. See the storm damage page.
Tidal or storm floodingTreated as contaminated. Crews handle it, not owners.
Sewage or drain overflowTreated as contaminated. Keep out and call.

Access and safety

If roads are wet, tell dispatch how you plan to reach the house and whether the causeway is passable. Follow any evacuation order and stay clear of floodwater. Do not touch the breaker panel while standing in water. Dispatch decides the crew plan once it knows the conditions.

For the mainland side of the causeway, see the Sconticut Neck page.

Insurance and records

Flood and sewer backup are often excluded from standard homeowners policies unless added, according to the Massachusetts Division of Insurance. Keep photos taken before cleanup, moisture logs and receipts for your insurer.
